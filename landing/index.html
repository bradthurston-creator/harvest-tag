How It Works

🏷️

1. Stick It

Peel a Harvest Tag sticker onto any clean jar lid. Works with all standard mason jars.

🎤

2. Tag It

Open the app. Speak or type what's inside — "Strawberry jam, June 2026." Tap your phone to the tag. Done.

📱

3. Tap It

Tap any tagged jar with your phone. The contents pop right up in the app.

The app keeps a searchable log of every jar you've tagged. Never wonder again.
